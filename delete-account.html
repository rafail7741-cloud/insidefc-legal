Delete Your InsideFC Account

Developer: Valon Visuals (Rafail Valountos), Munich, Germany
App: InsideFC (com.valonvisuals.fchome)

If you would like to delete your InsideFC account and the personal data associated with it, follow the steps below.

How To Request Deletion

Step 1. Send an email to contact@valonvisuals.com from the email address used to register your InsideFC account.
Step 2. Use the subject line: InsideFC account deletion request.
Step 3. Include the username you chose inside the app so we can locate your record.

We will process your request within 30 days and reply to the same email address to confirm completion.

What We Delete

  • Firebase Authentication account (email, sign-in identifiers)
  • User profile document (display name, username, profile photo URL)
  • Profile photo file in Cloud Storage
  • FCM push notification tokens
  • Username reservation entry
  • Subscription metadata stored in Firestore

What We Retain

  • Chat messages you posted in shared channels are retained according to the channel's retention policy (5 days for #general, 20 days for #investments, kept for #rules). Messages older than your deletion request will be removed when they reach their normal expiry; we may anonymize older messages so they remain in the channel history without identifying you.
  • Aggregated, non-identifying logs (for example, error counts) that cannot be linked back to you.
  • Records required by law (for example, billing receipts that are mandatory to keep for tax purposes under German law) for the period required by that law, after which they are deleted.

Subscription

If you have an active Google Play subscription, please cancel it through your Google Play subscriptions page before requesting account deletion: https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. Deleting your InsideFC account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription.

Questions

Contact contact@valonvisuals.com for any questions about this process or your data.
