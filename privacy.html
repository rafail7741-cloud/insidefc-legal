Privacy Policy — InsideFC

Last updated: 2026-05-13
Data controller: Valon Visuals (Rafail Valountos), Munich, Germany
Contact: contact@valonvisuals.com

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use InsideFC, why we collect it, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Email address (provided by you or by Google Sign-In)
  • Display name (provided by you or by Google Sign-In)
  • Profile photo (optional — uploaded by you, or imported from Google if available)
  • Firebase Authentication user ID (a random identifier)

When you use the chat:

  • Messages you send (text, GIF URLs, sent timestamp)
  • Notification token (FCM token) to deliver push notifications

When you read content:

  • Read receipts per channel (stored locally on your device)

We do NOT collect:

  • Your phone number, address, or payment information
  • Analytics tracking (no Google Analytics, no third-party tracking SDKs)
  • Location data
  • Contacts

2. Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Account creation and chat: performance of a contract (the service you requested)
  • Push notifications: your explicit consent (you can disable in system settings)
  • Moderation logs: legitimate interest in keeping the community safe

3. Where Data Is Stored

Data is stored on Google Firebase servers (Firestore: europe-west3; Cloud Storage: us-east1). Google acts as our data processor. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. How Long We Keep Data

  • Account data: as long as your account exists
  • Messages in #general: 5 days
  • Messages in #investments: 20 days
  • Messages in #rules: indefinitely
  • Posts (Leaks/News): indefinitely, until deleted by an admin
  • Profile photos: until you replace or delete your account
  • FCM tokens: rotated periodically; old tokens stay until manual cleanup

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing. Your display name, profile photo, and messages are visible to other signed-in users of the app, as required for chat to function.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate data (you can edit your own profile)
  • Erase your data — contact us to delete your account
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability — request an export
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g. BayLDA in Bavaria)

To exercise these rights, contact contact@valonvisuals.com.

7. Security

We use Firebase Authentication, Firebase Security Rules, and Firebase App Check to limit access to authorized clients. Connections use HTTPS. No system is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16. If you become aware that a minor has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third-Party Content

InsideFC includes a feature ("FCApp") that displays the official EA SPORTS FC Ultimate Team Web App inside a web view. When you use that feature, you are subject to EA's own terms and privacy policy at https://www.ea.com/legal. We do not have access to your EA account credentials or your activity inside the EA web app — that traffic is between you and EA directly. InsideFC is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Electronic Arts Inc.

10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions or data requests: contact@valonvisuals.com
